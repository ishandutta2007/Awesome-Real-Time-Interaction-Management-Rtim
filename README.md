# Awesome-Real-Time-Interaction-Management-Rtim

## Top Real-Time Interaction Management (RTIM) Tools Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Next-Best-Action Decisioning, In-the-Moment Personalization & Cross-Channel Orchestration*  

**Last updated: March 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Real-Time Interaction Management (RTIM) Tools**. These tools decide, in milliseconds, what message, offer, or action to present to a customer across web, mobile, email, contact center, and offline channels — using behavioral data, predictive models, and business rules to optimize engagement and customer lifetime value.



**Examples** include Salesforce Interaction Studio, Pegasystems Customer Decision Hub, Dynamic Yield, Bloomreach Personalization, Adobe Target, Redpoint rgOne, Thunderhead ONE, SAS Real-Time Decision Manager, Insider, and Sitecore Discover (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom decision logic, and transparent next-best-action engines — ideal for growth engineers, data scientists, and developers building data-sovereign interaction management platforms.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Salesforce Interaction Studio](https://www.salesforce.com/)**  

  Real-time interaction management solution that identifies audiences across channels, matches known and anonymous users, and delivers personalized next-best-actions at scale. Combines behavioral data, ML-powered A/B testing, and simulation to optimize conversions and loyalty .



- **[Pegasystems Customer Decision Hub](https://www.pega.com/)**  

  Enterprise "always-on brain" for next-best-action decisions across all channels. Consolidates disparate data and analytics into a unified platform to predict customer needs and deliver tailored 1:1 interactions. Includes solutions for complaints management, financial health coaching, and compassionate collections .



- **[Dynamic Yield](https://www.dynamicyield.com/)**  

  Personalization and experimentation platform with real-time decisioning, automated optimization, and A/B testing for e-commerce and content sites.



- **[Bloomreach Personalization](https://www.bloomreach.com/)**  

  AI-driven personalization for commerce with product recommendations, search, and content optimization.



- **[Adobe Target](https://business.adobe.com/products/target/adobe-target.html)**  

  Enterprise personalization and experimentation engine with AI-driven auto-targeting and automated personalization.



- **[Redpoint rgOne](https://www.redpointglobal.com/)**  

  Customer engagement hub that unifies customer data, determines next-best-actions in real time, and orchestrates interactions across all touchpoints. Delivers single customer view, advanced identity resolution, and contextually relevant messaging .



- **[Thunderhead ONE](https://www.thunderhead.com/)**  

  Real-time interaction management and journey orchestration platform (now part of Medallia). Brings together IoT, digital, contact center, and offline interactions to deliver individualized journeys for millions of customers across billions of touchpoints .



- **[SAS Real-Time Decision Manager](https://www.sas.com/)**  

  Enterprise decisioning platform for inbound and outbound interactions with real-time analytics and next-best-action capabilities.



- **[Insider](https://useinsider.com/)**  

  AI-powered personalization and customer engagement platform with predictive segments and individual-level experiences.



- **[Sitecore Discover](https://www.sitecore.com/)**  

  AI-powered search and product discovery with personalized recommendations for e-commerce experiences.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Dittofeed](https://github.com/dittofeed/dittofeed)**  

  Open-source customer engagement platform with developer tools, positioned as an alternative to Iterable and Customer.io. Features drag-and-drop journey builder, segmentation interface, web-based template editor, git-based workflows, testing SDK, OpenTelemetry integration, and self-hostable platform. MIT licensed .



- **[Nirnaya](https://github.com/nirnaya-ai/nirnaya)**  

  Typed decision engine that makes deterministic choices about text in a single forward pass. Given a state and a set of questions, returns an answer and calibrated probability for every allowed outcome. Supports choice (2-10 options), yes/no, and score (2-26 ordered levels) question types. 421M parameter model with MLX and PyTorch runtimes. Includes HTTP server with Ollama-compatible API .



- **[PEARL](https://zenodo.org/records/22236901)**  

  Source code for next-best-action selection with intervention misalignment measurement. Implements within-patient preference-pair construction, cross-fitted outcome and propensity models, a pessimistic support-restricted routing rule, epsilon threshold sensitivity sweep, and decision-curve/net-benefit analysis. Designed for care management with synthetic-data demonstration .



- **[Mkt5 next-best-action](https://github.com/portable-genai/next-best-action)**  

  Generic, multi-vertical next-best-action engine for banking and online retail. Given a customer and offer catalog, produces ranked, fully cited recommendations with deterministic, unit-tested engines for candidate filtering, eligibility/suitability, and ranking. LLM (Gemini) only explains "why recommended." Supports local, GCP, and on-prem profiles .



- **[dot-agora](https://github.com/dot-agora/dot-agora)**  

  Mediated conversations between humans, LLM assistants, and tools. Puts a mediator between every participant with protocols for approval, voting, and policies as plain values. Journal is the only state — permissions, approvals, and votes are rules of the room. Supports fork/resume from journal, LLM Assistant with tool discovery, and REST/SSE server .



- **[Gorse](https://github.com/gorse-io/gorse)**  

  AI-powered open-source recommender system engine. Automatically trains models from user-item interaction data to generate personalized recommendations. Features multi-source recommendations, multimodal embedding support, GUI dashboard, and RESTful APIs. Apache 2.0 licensed.



- **[Metarank](https://github.com/metarank/metarank)**  

  Open-source low-code ML personalized ranking service. Integrates customer signals into ranking to optimize CTR, tracks visitor profiles for real-time personalization, and supports LLMs for semantic search. Optimized for 10-20ms reranking latency.



- **[GrowthBook](https://github.com/growthbook/growthbook)**  

  Open-source feature flagging and A/B testing platform with advanced targeting, gradual rollouts, and world-class experiment stats engine (CUPED, Sequential, Bayesian, Bandits). MIT licensed.



- **[Quick Connect](https://github.com/dramaticoutr/quick-connect)**  

  Open-source customer communication platform with real-time chat, stories, management dashboard, notifications, and file handler. Cloud-native architecture with support agents and admin roles. AGPL-3.0 licensed .



- **[live-q-a-with-real-time-voting](https://github.com/mahesararslan/Live-Q-A-with-Real-Time-Voting)**  

  Real-time interactive Q&A platform with live voting. Features room-based questions, upvoting, admin controls for marking answered, and scalable architecture with Redis Pub/Sub. Next.js frontend and NestJS backend .



- **[menti](https://github.com/Chad-007/menti)**  

  Real-time interactive quiz and polling application inspired by Mentimeter. Uses Redis for authentication and WebSockets for real-time quiz interactions. MIT licensed .



- **[AI Business Coach](https://github.com/HKtrill/ai-business-coach)**  

  Local-first interpretable ML cascade for transparent, abstention-aware customer decision intelligence. Features glass-box inference with stage-level traceability: calibrated logistic regression, symbolic rule router, explainable boosting machine, and weighted-confidence arbiter. Designed for customer-level prediction with full auditability .



- **[Laudspeaker](https://github.com/laudspeaker/laudspeaker)**  

  Open-source customer engagement and product onboarding platform. Alternative to Braze, OneSignal, Customer.io, Appcues, and Pendo. Design product onboarding flows and send event-triggered emails, SMS, push, and more .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[EdgePersona](https://github.com/Crynge/EdgePersona)** — Real-time cross-channel personalization engine and edge CDP with multi-armed bandits and Bayesian A/B testing.

- **[Experimently](https://github.com/getexperimently/experimently)** — Open-source experimentation platform with sequential and Bayesian statistics, per-flag safety monitoring, and automatic rollback.

- **[Bucketeer](https://github.com/bucketeer-io/bucketeer)** — Enterprise-grade feature management and experimentation platform with no usage limits.

- **[ReflexUI](https://github.com/mmarqueti/reflexui)** — Adaptive, explainable screens driven by fast typed-judgment models with Redis-backed decision store.

- **Vector Databases** — Qdrant, Weaviate, Milvus for semantic recommendation retrieval.

- **Feature Stores** — Feast for serving real-time ML features to decision engines.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Dittofeed** for engagement orchestration, **Nirnaya** for typed decision inference, **Gorse** for recommendations, **GrowthBook** for experimentation, and **Redis + PostgreSQL** for low-latency profile storage to build a complete self-hosted RTIM stack.
